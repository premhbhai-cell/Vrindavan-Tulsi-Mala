Yes. Most of our spiritual products are suitable for daily prayers, meditation, temple visits, devotional practices, and regular wear. Please refer to the individual product page for any specific care or usage information.

Are these spiritual products suitable for gifting?

Absolutely. Our spiritual collection makes a meaningful gift for festivals, housewarming ceremonies, birthdays, weddings, religious occasions, and anyone who values devotion and spirituality.
